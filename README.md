# 💫 About Me:
I am a Computer Science undergraduate at **FAST–NUCES**, graduating in 2026, with a strong focus on **AI/ML Engineering** and **Full-Stack Development**. <br>I specialize in building things bottom-up — from custom Transformers for NLP tasks to RAG pipelines, GANs, and production-ready GenAI systems, backed by full-stack apps built with the MERN stack, Next.js, FastAPI, and Flask.

My hands-on work includes a **RAG-based Clinical Diagnostic System** (FAISS + LangChain over 500+ clinical notes), a **Smart Healthcare Assistant** with CT/X-ray analysis and 3D visualization, **GAN-based image generation & translation** (DCGAN, WGAN-GP, Pix2Pix, CycleGAN), an **Urdu Conversational Chatbot** using custom multi-head attention Transformers, and a **Pseudo-Code to Code** decoder-only Transformer. I also completed a Generative AI internship at **DecodeLabs** and a C/C++ internship at **TechnoHacks EduTech**.<br><br>
I enjoy turning research-level ideas into practical, production-ready solutions using Python, PyTorch, and JavaScript. I am a motivated learner who values continuous growth, collaboration, and innovation, and I am actively seeking internships, projects, and research opportunities in AI/ML and full-stack engineering.
